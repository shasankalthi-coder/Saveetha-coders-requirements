About SIMATS Engineering

Engineer to Excel

SIMATS Engineering is part of Saveetha Institute of Medical and Technical Sciences. The institution focuses on engineering education, research, practical learning, industry interaction and innovation.


The institution provides undergraduate and postgraduate engineering education along with research opportunities. Students are encouraged to develop practical skills through projects, laboratories, internships and capstone projects.
